About Melodie

THE
FACE
BEHIND

Based in Maryland, I'm a 25-year-old Cameroonian-American model at the start of an intentional modeling journey. Full of presence, energy, and a genuine love for the craft. I move through the world with curiosity as I train in Afro dance and kickboxing, solving cryptic puzzles, playing chess, tutoring students, and seeking out the places most people overlook. My eye is editorial, my ambition is runway. I bring discipline, culture, and an authentic point of view to every frame.

Seeking first agency representation and test shoots. Fully committed, coachable, and ready to work.
